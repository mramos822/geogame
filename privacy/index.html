Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2025  ·  Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how myGeoChallenge ("we", "us", or "our"), operated at mygeochallenge.com, collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website and game. By using myGeoChallenge you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information depending on how you use the game:

  • Account information: If you create an account, we collect your username and email address. A profile photo is optional.
  • Game data: Scores, high scores, play counts, rank titles, and session activity stored to provide leaderboard and profile features.
  • Social data: Friend lists, friend requests sent/received, and in-game emotes exchanged during Versus matches. This data is stored to power the social features of the game.
  • Technical data: Browser type, device type, operating system, and IP address collected automatically when you visit the site.
  • Guest usage: If you play as a guest (without an account), no personal information is collected. Your session data is temporary and not stored on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and continuously improve the game and its features.
  • To display global leaderboards and track player rankings.
  • To enable social features such as friend lists, Versus match invitations, and in-game notifications.
  • To serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense.
  • To remember your language preference (English or Spanish) across sessions.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our terms.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our site. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site or other websites.

Ads are displayed outside the game interface and are never placed inside game screens or panels. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland, we use Google's Consent Management Platform to request your consent before serving personalized ads. You can change your consent preferences at any time.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We use cookies and browser local storage for the following purposes:

  • Authentication: To keep you logged in to your account across sessions (via Supabase session tokens).
  • Preferences: To remember your language choice (EN/ES), mute settings, and other in-game preferences.
  • Advertising: Google AdSense uses cookies to measure ad performance and serve personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your consent.
  • Analytics: Basic usage data may be collected to understand how players interact with the game (e.g., which modes are most played).

You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect authentication and some game features.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — Authentication, database, real-time updates, and file storage (profile photos). Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense — Advertising platform. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — Web fonts served from Google's CDN. Google may log requests. Google Privacy Policy.

6. Public Data and Leaderboards

If you create an account, your username, profile photo, rank title, and high scores are visible to other players on global leaderboards and in Versus match lobbies. This is an inherent part of the competitive features of the game.

You can control your display name at any time from your in-game profile panel. If you wish to remove your public data entirely, you may request account deletion (see Section 8).

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account remains active. Game scores and leaderboard entries associated with your account are stored indefinitely to maintain ranking integrity.

If you delete your account, your personal information (email, username, profile photo) will be permanently removed within 30 days. Anonymized aggregate data (e.g., total play counts) may be retained for analytical purposes.

Guest session data is not stored on our servers and is discarded when the browser session ends.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update or correct your username and profile photo at any time from within the game.
  • Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data.
  • Portability: Request an export of your personal data in a machine-readable format.
  • Opt-out of personalized ads: Withdraw your advertising consent at any time via Google Ads Settings or your browser's cookie controls.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at mygeochallengeapp@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

California residents (CCPA): You have the right to know what personal data we collect, the right to delete it, and the right to opt out of the sale of your data. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

EEA/UK residents (GDPR): You have the right to object to processing, restrict processing, and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Children's Privacy

myGeoChallenge is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). The game can be played as a guest without providing any personal information.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at mygeochallengeapp@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. All data is stored and transmitted via secure, encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

Authentication is handled by Supabase, which uses industry-standard security practices including hashed password storage and secure session tokens. However, no system is completely immune to security risks, and we encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

11. International Data Transfers

myGeoChallenge is operated from the United States. If you are accessing the game from outside the U.S., your data may be transferred to and processed in the U.S. or other countries where our service providers (such as Supabase) operate.

These transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, including Supabase's compliance with applicable data protection frameworks. By using myGeoChallenge, you consent to this transfer.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. We will notify users of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of myGeoChallenge after a policy update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

13. Contact

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us:

  • Email: mygeochallengeapp@gmail.com
  • Website: mygeochallenge.com
