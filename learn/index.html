Flags Worth Memorizing First

These distinctive flags appear in Suitcase Shuffle and are almost guaranteed correct answers once you recognize them — they have no close look-alikes.

Switzerland
Only square national flag. Red with white cross — impossible to confuse.
Jamaica
Gold diagonal cross on black and green. No other flag uses this combo.
Mexico
Green, white, red — with a golden eagle on cactus in the center stripe.
Brazil
Yellow diamond on green. The shape alone gives it away in South America.
South Africa
Six colors including a horizontal Y-shape. Most colorful flag on the continent.
Ukraine
Two stripes: blue (sky) over yellow (wheat). Simple and vivid.
Bangladesh
Green with offset red circle. Like Japan but with green background and shifted circle.
Cambodia
Only national flag featuring a building: Angkor Wat temple in the center.

Learn by Color Family

Most flags use 2–3 colors. Grouping by color scheme is the fastest elimination strategy in Suitcase Shuffle:

  • Red, White & Blue tricolor — France, Netherlands, Russia, Czech Republic, Slovakia, Slovenia, Chile, Cuba. The stripe direction and arrangement are the key differentiators.
  • Red, White & Green — Italy, Mexico, Hungary, Bulgaria, Belarus, Maldives, Iran. Look for emblems in the center stripe.
  • Pan-African (red, yellow, green) — Ethiopia, Ghana, Guinea, Mali, Cameroon, Senegal. Stars and stripe arrangements vary.
  • Pan-Arab (red, white, black, green) — Jordan, Iraq, Syria, UAE, Yemen. Most use horizontal stripes; central emblems differ.
  • Nordic Cross — Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland, Iceland. Off-center cross on a plain field. Instantly recognizable once you know the pattern.

Landmark Recognition Shortcuts

  • Architectural style is your first clue. Islamic geometric patterns → Middle East/North Africa. Tiered stone temples → Southeast Asia. Classical columns → Mediterranean Europe.
  • Know these country associations: Hagia Sophia → Turkey (not Greece). Angkor Wat → Cambodia (not Thailand). Machu Picchu → Peru (not Bolivia).
  • UNESCO sites dominate the list. Familiarizing yourself with the World Heritage list by region prepares you for the majority of Landmark Loco questions.
  • Natural wonders have their own logic — Victoria Falls (Zambia/Zimbabwe), Iguazú (Argentina/Brazil), Uluru (Australia). Pay attention to the country attribution, not just the landmark name.

Capitals That Aren't the Biggest City

These surprise capitals are frequently tested in City Blitz and often catch players off guard:

  • Australia: Canberra (not Sydney or Melbourne)
  • Canada: Ottawa (not Toronto)
  • Brazil: Brasília (not São Paulo or Rio — Brasília was purpose-built in 1960)
  • USA: Washington D.C. (not New York City)
  • India: New Delhi (not Mumbai)
  • Nigeria: Abuja (not Lagos)
  • Pakistan: Islamabad (not Karachi)
  • South Africa: Three capitals — Pretoria (executive), Cape Town (legislative), Bloemfontein (judicial)
  • Tanzania: Dodoma (not Dar es Salaam)
  • Kazakhstan: Astana (formerly Nur-Sultan — not Almaty)
