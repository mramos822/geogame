How This Actually Started

Slightly embarrassing origin: we were watching a sports broadcast and someone pointed at a flag and asked what country it was. Nobody at the table knew. We searched for something to practice this and everything we found was either boring flashcard sites, apps you had to pay for, or things last updated years ago.

So we built our own. Just flags, that's it. Then we added country shapes. Then city locations on a live map. Then landmark photos. Then real-time Versus. Then 35 rank titles because we genuinely couldn't stop (we have a problem).

The game keeps getting better. The version you're playing today is meaningfully better than the one we launched. The next will be better than this one — that's the only promise we'll make.

Things We'll Be Straight About

💰 How We Make Money (And Don't)

Free and will stay free. Supported by Google ads in the page margins — never inside gameplay, never during questions. Also have a Ko-fi page if you want to support voluntarily. You don't owe us anything — sharing the game helps just as much.

🛠️ What We Are

Basically one person who can't stop adding features. We ship bugs. We fix them. We sometimes break things we just fixed. The game you play today is noticeably better than what we launched, and we're not slowing down.

🔒 Your Data

Guest play: we store nothing. With an account: we save your username, email, and scores — that's it. No data selling, no marketing emails. Full details in the Privacy Policy.

🗺️ What's Coming

In progress: full 1v1 campaign Versus (both players run all 4 modes simultaneously), more landmark photos (90 is good, 150+ is the goal), better mobile performance. No dates promised — we do this alongside real jobs. But we're not going anywhere.
