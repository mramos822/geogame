Geography made addictively fun

myGeoChallenge is a free browser-based geography quiz game designed for players of all ages. Whether you're a student studying for a geography exam, a trivia fan, or just someone who loves exploring the world from your couch — there's a mode for you.

Every correct answer earns points, and every fast answer earns even more with our speed bonus system. Scores are tracked globally so you can always see exactly where you rank compared to players worldwide.

Create a free account to track your high scores across all four modes, challenge friends in real-time Versus matches, and earn one of 35 unique rank titles — starting from "Agoraphobic" and climbing all the way to globe-trotting legend.

Play the full World Tour campaign to face all four modes back-to-back, or use Practice Tour to study a specific continent or difficulty at your own pace. Fully bilingual — English and Spanish — with no downloads required.
