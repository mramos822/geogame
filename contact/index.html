Before you write about a bug

You'll get a fix much faster if you include three things:

  • What you were doing — which game mode, and at what moment it happened.
  • Your device and browser — e.g. "iPhone 13, Safari" or "Windows 11, Chrome".
  • A screenshot if the problem is visual. It's worth a thousand words, literally.

Account & privacy

Want your account or data deleted? Email us from the address linked to your account and we'll take care of it. Details on exactly what we store (very little) are in the Privacy Policy.
