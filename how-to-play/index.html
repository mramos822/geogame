Game Modes

4 Modes. All Different. All Hard.

Each mode hits a completely different skill set. Flags are pattern recognition. Shapes are spatial memory. Cities are speed and confidence. Landmarks are cultural knowledge. You'll be good at one right away and bad at the others. That's normal.

Mode 01
Suitcase Shuffle
Flags & Countries

How it works

Suitcases roll down an airport carousel, each belonging to a different country. Your job is to match the correct flag to each bag before the 8-second clock runs out.

Six answer options are shown at first. Wrong options fade out progressively to help you narrow it down — 6 choices → 4 → 2. Clicking a faded option does nothing.

A correct answer scores points. A wrong answer or timeout scores zero. The speed bonus multiplier builds with consecutive correct answers — miss one and it resets.

Mode 02
Map Mayhem
Country Shapes

How it works

A country's silhouette appears on screen and you must identify it from four options. No labels, no map context — just the shape.

Shapes range from instantly recognizable (Italy's boot, Japan's islands) to surprisingly tricky (neighboring African nations, small Central European countries).

This mode draws from all six inhabited continents. African and Southeast Asian country shapes tend to be the hardest — a good reason to drill those regions in Practice Tour first.

Mode 03
City Blitz
World Cities

How it works

A city name appears at the top. An interactive world map fills the screen. Click the country where that city is located. Speed matters — the faster you click, the bigger your bonus.

Cities are drawn from three difficulty tiers that unlock progressively. Get 5 right → Medium cities unlock. Get 15 right → Hard cities unlock (Ashgabat, Paramaribo, Dili…).

You don't need to click the exact city dot — clicking anywhere in the correct country region is usually enough to score the point.

Mode 04
Landmark Loco
World Landmarks

How it works

A landmark photo appears on screen. You select the correct answer from four options — each option shows a "Landmark, Country" pair. The combined pair is the answer unit.

Landmarks include UNESCO World Heritage Sites, architectural icons, natural wonders, and cultural monuments from every continent — from the Eiffel Tower to Angkor Wat.

Architectural style is often the biggest clue. Islamic geometric patterns → Middle East or North Africa. Tiered stone temples → Southeast Asia. Classical columns → Mediterranean.

Campaign

World Tour — The Real Test

All 4 modes back-to-back, one combined score, one rank title at the end. This is what the leaderboard is based on. You can't just be good at flags — all four count.

→ → → →

Your score accumulates across all four modes. At the end, you receive a final World Tour score and a rank title based on total performance — this rank appears on the global World Tour leaderboard.

Topping the World Tour leaderboard requires consistent performance across all four modes. You can't coast on one favorite — all four count equally.

Multiplayer

Versus — Real-Time PvP

Versus mode lets you battle friends in real time. Both players see the same questions in the same order (seeded for fairness) and answer simultaneously. Live scores show on screen the whole time.

To start a Versus match, you need a free account. Invite a friend directly from the social panel in the loading screen. Send emotes during the match — they only trigger on your opponent's wrong answers, so no spam during streaks.

  • Same questions for both players — no unfair advantages
  • Live score comparison on screen at all times
  • Win/Loss tracked in your profile history
  • Emotes: 5 reaction options available mid-match
Study Mode

Practice Tour — No Pressure

Practice Tour removes the game-over timer. You answer at your own pace and focus on whatever area you want to improve. It's the best way to prepare before a competitive run.

Filter by continent, difficulty, or mode — then drill exactly the questions that will help you most. Practice Tour scores don't count toward global leaderboards.

  • Continents: Africa · Asia · Europe · Americas · Oceania
  • Difficulties: Easy · Medium · Hard
  • Any of the 4 modes available in Practice
  • No time limit — answer when you're ready
Daily Challenge

GlobeQuiz — Guess the Country, No Clock

A random country is picked every time you play — find it on a 3D globe you can drag, spin, and zoom, by typing country names as your guesses. No multiple choice here: you actually have to know your geography.

Every guess shows the distance in kilometers to the real answer, plus a "getting warmer/colder" hint compared to your last try. Unlimited attempts, no game-over timer — just a stopwatch tracking how fast you nail it.

  • Daily streak: win at least once a day to keep it going — resets at midnight New York time for everyone, no matter your timezone
  • Friends can spectate live — they see the same globe and your guesses landing in real time
  • A new random country every single game — replay as many times as you want, never the same twice
  • Runs separate from World Tour scoring — its own streak, not part of the combined campaign rank
